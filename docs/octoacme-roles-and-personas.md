# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategy. They collaborate with product and engineering to validate acceptance criteria, identify quality risks, and ensure releases meet quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with features and milestones
- Define acceptance criteria validation approach
- Execute manual QA and coordinate automated testing coverage
- Report quality metrics and flag quality risks
- Collaborate on bug triage and regression testing before releases

### Goals
- Maximize customer confidence in product quality
- Reduce production defects through systematic testing
- Establish clear quality metrics and traceability

### Typical Communication
- Acceptance criteria reviews with Product Managers and Developers
- Daily quality status updates in standups
- QA sign-off on features before release

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with **Developers** to understand implementation details and design comprehensive tests
- Partners with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria interpretation and validation strategy
- Reports to **Project Managers** on schedule impact of quality issues and blockers

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide strategic technical guidance, conduct design reviews, and make system-level decisions. They help teams navigate complex technical trade-offs and reduce technical risk.

### Responsibilities
- Participate in technical design reviews and spike investigations
- Identify and escalate technical risks and dependencies
- Guide architectural decisions and technology choices
- Mentor developers on best practices and code quality
- Ensure technical feasibility of roadmap items

### Goals
- Maintain system quality and long-term maintainability
- Accelerate delivery by reducing rework and technical debt
- Transfer knowledge and grow team technical capabilities

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions and architecture discussions
- Technical spike reviews and recommendations
- Code review feedback and guidance

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Advises **Developers** on design and implementation approaches to minimize rework
- Partners with **Product Managers** on feasibility assessments and technical trade-offs
- Supports **Project Managers** with technical risk assessment and dependency identification

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic alignment, and decision authority. They ensure projects deliver business value and have necessary resources and approvals.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and success metrics interpretation
- Approve major scope changes and milestone slips
- Allocate resources and resolve team conflicts
- Receive status updates and escalations
- Validate that delivered outcomes meet business objectives

### Goals
- Ensure business value realization from projects
- Maintain strategic alignment and resource efficiency
- Mitigate business and organizational risks

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder update briefings
- Ad-hoc escalation and decision requests
- Milestone reviews and release announcements

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Provides guidance and approvals to **Project Managers** on scope and priority decisions
- Reviews success metrics and business outcomes with **Product Managers**
- Serves as escalation point for team blockers requiring executive-level decisions

---

## DevOps/Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and release coordination. They enable safe, repeatable, and observable deployments to production.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Coordinate release activities and deployment scheduling
- Execute or automate deployments to staging and production
- Monitor infrastructure and post-deployment health
- Support incident response and rollback procedures

### Goals
- Minimize deployment risk and production incidents
- Reduce time-to-production for features
- Maintain system reliability and observability

### Typical Communication
- Release coordination meetings before deployments
- Infrastructure and deployment metrics reviews
- Incident response collaboration

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with **Developers** on CI/CD requirements and deployment feedback
- Coordinates with **Product Managers** on release timing and feature rollout strategy
- Works with **Project Managers** on release planning, schedules, and deployment windows
